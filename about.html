About Us

We keep Nigeria moving, reliable transport and logistics, one road at a time.

Who We Are

FG Transport & Logistics is a child company of FG Onyenwe Enterprises Nigeria Limited, founded in 1975 and registered with the CAC under Companies Allied Act (CAMA).

We are a homegrown transportation powerhouse built on a simple promise: to keep Nigeria moving. We understand that whether you are traveling to see family or sending a vital shipment to a business partner, you need a service that is reliable, honest, and deeply familiar with the Nigerian terrain.

Backed by a culture of excellence, we navigate Africa’s evolving transport landscape with a premium fleet of luxury passenger vehicles and over 200 haulage trucks.

We have brought our passenger transport, haulage, logistics and delivery services under one roof to make life easier for you. No complications, no hassle, just dependable service from people who know the road.

What We Do

Travel Services

We provide safe and comfortable road travel across Nigeria. Our fleet is maintained to the highest standards, and our drivers are experienced professionals who prioritize your safety from the moment you board at our terminal until you reach your destination.

Logistics & Delivery to Freight & Haulage

We handle your goods with the same care we give our passengers. From small shipments to large commercial goods, our logistics network ensures your items are delivered safely.

Our Mission

To move people and parcels across Nigeria with safety, reliability, and integrity.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted partner for movement in every Nigerian home and business.

The FG Difference
