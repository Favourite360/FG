Prohibited Items

Items that cannot be shipped

⚠️ Disclaimer

These items are prohibited by law or FG Transport & Logistics' Terms and Conditions of Carriage. The list is not exhaustive and may be updated. Shipping any prohibited item is a material breach of FG Transport & Logistics' Terms, and FG Transport & Logistics bears no liability for loss or damage to such items.

This is a general prohibited items list. Please note that FG Transport & Logistics will check and verify every cargo and passenger goods to ensure guidelines are met within Nigeria and the West African Coast we operate in. We advise customers to confirm country-specific restrictions before shipping.

Shipping Prohibited Items

The following items cannot be shipped under any circumstances:

Most Common Items

Shipping of legal currency, banknotes, coins, and traveller's cheques is strictly prohibited.
Loose precious or semi-precious stones and raw gold cannot be shipped due to security and regulatory reasons.
Counterfeit documents and fake currency are strictly prohibited and illegal.
Most perishable food items cannot be shipped safely due to preservation and health concerns.
All narcotics and illegal drugs are strictly prohibited under international drug control laws.
All illicit drugs and their derivatives are completely prohibited.

Hazardous & Dangerous Items

Flammable or corrosive goods pose serious safety risks and are prohibited for transport.
All firearms, ammunition, explosives, and replicas are strictly prohibited for security reasons.
Poisonous substances, acids, and pressurized gas cylinders are dangerous and prohibited.
Bladed items and sharp weapons are prohibited for safety reasons.
Party poppers and Christmas crackers are prohibited due to their explosive nature.

Human, Animal & Plant Materials

Animal trophies and remains are prohibited due to environmental and ethical regulations.
Animal ashes and untested by-products are prohibited for health and safety reasons.
Clinical and biological samples are prohibited due to health and biohazard concerns.
Live animals and insects cannot be shipped due to welfare and safety concerns.
Human remains and ashes are prohibited and require specialized funeral services.
Certain flowers and plants may be restricted due to import/export regulations.

Restricted/Regulated Goods

Pharmaceutical products may be shipped only under strict regulatory conditions and proper approvals.
Drones require special regulatory approvals before they can be shipped.
Military equipment and wares require special authorization and approvals.
Alcohol in commercial quantities requires special regulatory conditions and approvals.
Tobacco products require special regulatory approvals for shipment.
Used clothing in bale form may require special regulatory conditions.
Used foreign furniture may be subject to import/export restrictions.

Alcohol, Cigarettes & Narcotics

Alcohol is allowed within Nigeria; however, restrictions may apply internationally and across West Africa.
Cigarettes, cigars, and tobacco products are strictly prohibited due to import/export restrictions.
Heroin is a controlled narcotic and is completely prohibited.
Methylamphetamine is an illegal stimulant and is strictly prohibited.
Cocaine is an illegal stimulant and is strictly prohibited.
Marijuana and cannabis-derived products are prohibited under international drug control laws.
Electronic smoking devices are strictly prohibited.

Other Prohibited Items

Counterfeit goods are prohibited due to intellectual property rights violations.
Offensive bladed weapons are prohibited for security and safety reasons.
All illicit drugs and their derivatives are strictly prohibited.
Pornography in any format is prohibited due to legal and moral concerns.
Active tracking devices are prohibited for security and privacy reasons.
