Industries we serve

Built for the sectors that cannot stop

Our expertise spans the most demanding sectors, where reliable, large-scale transportation is fundamental to business growth.

Manufacturing & Industrial Goods

Machinery, bulk components and finished goods moved between factories, ports and distribution centres, on schedules your production line can plan around.

Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)

High-volume, time-sensitive runs from production lines to central warehouses and regional distributors, so stock reaches the shelf before demand does.

Construction & Building Materials

Heavy and oversized loads — cement, steel, timber and plant — delivered to site when the build schedule calls for them.

Agriculture & Food Distribution

Produce, grain and packaged foods moved from farms and processors to markets nationwide, with refrigerated units available for temperature-sensitive loads.

Technology & Electronics

Servers, devices and sensitive electronics handled with secure loading, careful packing and tracked delivery to offices, retailers and data facilities.

Automotive & Heavy Equipment

Vehicles, spare parts and heavy plant equipment carried on flatbeds and lowbeds built for the weight and the dimensions.

Things you need to know

Before you ship with us

Essential information to ensure your shipment experience is smooth and hassle-free.

01

Prohibited Items

Safety is paramount. We do not transport flammable liquids, contraband, perishable raw foods without proper packaging, or fragile items that are not crated.

02

Pick-up Policy

The receiver is notified by SMS once the item arrives. They must present a valid ID and the tracking number to claim the package at the destination terminal.

03

Insurance

We treat every item with care. For high-value items such as electronics or jewellery, we recommend declaring the full value for insurance coverage during booking.

04

Customer Support

Our support team is available to guide you through booking, tracking and claims.
