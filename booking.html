Reliable service

Things you need to know

Luggage Allowance

To ensure comfort for everyone, every ticket comes with a standard luggage limit. For extra heavy loads, our terminal team will assist with weighing and excess charges or transfer to Logistics service.

Arrival Time

Please arrive at the terminal at least 30 minutes before your scheduled departure for check-in and luggage tagging.

Identification

Bring a valid ID (National ID, Voter’s Card, or Driver’s License) for manifest and security checks.

Rescheduling & Refunds

You can reschedule if you notify us at least 4 hours before departure. Missed trips (no-shows) are non-refundable.

Children & Infants

Children needing their own seat are charged full fare. Infants on laps travel free — inform the terminal manager on arrival.

Our Safety Measures

Speed Limiters

All our vehicles are installed with official speed-limiting devices to ensure safe highway speeds.

Vehicle Tracking

We monitor the location and speed of every bus in our fleet in real-time from our headquarters.

Routine Maintenance

Every vehicle undergoes a mechanical check before it is allowed to load passengers.
